- ink: #11231c

## Type
- Display: bold condensed sans for headlines (heavy weight, tight tracking)
- Body: clean grotesque sans
- Eyebrow: 0.68rem, weight 900, letter-spacing 0.18em, uppercase

## Logos (official — use these, never the generic app icon)
- Prefer transparent PNGs: `mobile/assets/alipo-mark.png`, `mobile/assets/alipo-lockup.png`
- JPG sources remain: `mobile/assets/alipo-mark.jpg`, `mobile/assets/alipo-lockup.jpg`
- Brand Asset keys: `brand-alipo-mark`, `brand-alipo-lockup`
- Rule: ONE logo per poster — either mark OR lockup, never both stacked

## Launch CTA hierarchy
- Primary CTA: `alipo.co.mw` (most prominent after/alongside headline)
- Secondary footer branding is optional and small

## Voice
- Civic utility, confident, local to Malawi
- Brand name "Alipo" means "it is available / it is there" in Chichewa
- URL: alipo.co.mw
